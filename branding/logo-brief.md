# Roastbook logo concept

A minimal mark combining an open journal with a coffee bean at its center.
The curved pages echo the bean’s shape, connecting coffee and journaling
in one recognizable symbol.

Use dark coffee brown (`#3a2a1e`) on warm cream (`#f6eddf`), with simple
shapes and enough weight to stay legible as a small GitHub avatar.
Keep the avatar free of text; pair the mark with a bold Bricolage Grotesque
wordmark for larger placements.

This brief describes a proposed logo, not a finished logo asset.
