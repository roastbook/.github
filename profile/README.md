**Better coffee, one brew at a time.**

We build open-source tools for people who love coffee and want to
understand what makes a great cup.

Our main project, **Roastbook**, is a self-hosted coffee journal.
Keep your brews, beans, recipes, gear, and café discoveries together,
with your data on your own infrastructure.

### Your daily coffee companion

- **Remember the good cups.** Log brew parameters, tasting notes,
  and ratings so you can make them again.
- **Get to know your coffee.** Track coffees and individual bags,
  from roast date to the last dose.
- **Find your next adjustment.** Explore trends across beans,
  brewing methods, and equipment.
- **Bring AI if you want it.** Scan coffee labels, research roasters,
  and get brew recommendations through your own AI provider.
- **Keep brewing offline.** Install the app, log without a connection,
  and sync when you reconnect.
- **Make it yours.** Self-host with Docker or Helm, and connect your
  own tools through the REST API and MCP server.
